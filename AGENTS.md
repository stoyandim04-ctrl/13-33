# Notes for agents working on this repo

- Bulgarian first, English second. Structured copy lives twice in `src/data/content.ts` (`useContent()`); every other visible string is written in Bulgarian in the component and wrapped in `tr()` from `src/lib/i18n.tsx`, with its English in `src/lib/en.ts` (the Bulgarian text is the key — a missing key shows Bulgarian). The switch is in the header; the choice is kept in localStorage and sets `<html lang>`. Manrope carries the Bulgarian Cyrillic forms — only switch to fonts with `cyrl/BGR` locl forms. The studio panel (`/studio`) stays Bulgarian. The gallery title is drawn into a canvas texture; it gets `lang` so the Bulgarian forms apply there too.
- Never invent clients, results or testimonials. Concept projects stay labelled (`status: "concept"`). The one time promise is the studio's own: the first version reaches the client's inbox within 4 working days of the deposit (`FIRST_VERSION_DAYS`). The word „основател“/founder never appears on the public site; we speak as the team.
- Visual system lives in `src/index.css` (`@theme`): the logo's blue on near-black — background #080A0F (two close shades), electric blue #246BFF (the one action colour), cyan #44C8FF (accent: labels, edges), white text, cool grey #8A93A6 for quiet text, white hairlines at 10%. Light only as soft blue/cyan glow in the 3D scene; no other colours (the old purple #8F7BFF is gone). Text in Manrope; the big words in the display serif from `src/lib/display-font.ts` (Playfair A by default, Cormorant B; `?font=a|b` switches for review) — both have the `cyrl/BGR` forms.
- The primary action is always the `RainbowLink`/`RainbowButton` (`components/ui/rainbow-button.tsx`, now a blue pill), one per view; other buttons lift on hover (`components/ui/button.tsx`), menu links use `.nav-link`.
- Home is one scroll story, the 3D gallery (`components/gallery/`; brief in Notion „Новият сайт на 13:33 — 3D галерия от проекти“): the title „Превръщаме идеите / в дигитални продукти“ with a ribbon of our sites running through it (in front of and behind the words) → the ribbon curls around the camera → it pulls away → the windows fall in and snap into a wall (hover/tap shows „[01] Име — тип · етикет — Виж“, click opens the live site in a new tab) → glitch → „За нас“ with a globe of windows → the 3D logo comes forward and flies back to the header → „Обсъди идеята си“ to `/zapochni`. The stretches are in `timeline.ts` (shared by the scene and the HTML layers); the anchors `#proekti`, `#za-nas`, `#kontakt` sit inside the section. Every window plays a clip only when it matters (a small `<video>` pool); the others show posters. Without WebGL, on a weak/slow device or with reduced motion → `gallery-lite.tsx` (same story as cards). `?3d` / `?lite` force a version. Very few words; no "why us", no FAQ: the chat answers questions.
- Projects live in `src/data/projects.ts` (the five confirmed on 21.10.2026: Варовик, Цукиби, OBSIDIAN No. 01, Black Clove, VELION LAB — no client or fitness sites). Their gallery windows are `public/gallery/<slug>-<n>.{webp,mp4,webm}`, recorded from the live sites by `npm run media:gallery [slug]`; a new project = one entry + that command. Dispose every three.js object, listener and video on unmount. `components/ui/works-wheel.tsx`, `grid-hero.tsx`, `horizon-*` and `components/sections/*` are the previous home page, kept unused.
- No secrets in client code: only `VITE_*` public values. Inquiries/AI/payments go through the server routes in `api/` (Supabase service key, Anthropic key, Stripe and Resend keys live only there). The AI consultant prices the project itself, item by item, inside `PRICE_GUIDE` (`src/lib/inquiry-shared.ts`, kept low on purpose); the server checks the total and sets the deposit to `DEPOSIT_SHARE` of it (15%, rounded to 5 €). Without a computed price the deposit falls back to `DEPOSITS` by project type (0 = no payment step). The price and deposit are shown on the client's page and paid there through Stripe. Every client message in the chat is forwarded to the studio's Telegram. No chat between client and studio after that: the team writes by e-mail.
- Before finishing: `npm run build` (runs `tsc -b`).
